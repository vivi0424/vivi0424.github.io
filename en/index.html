About

Between linguistics and data

I’m a master’s student in Language Technologies in Business and Education at HSE University — St. Petersburg and a research intern at the Language Convergence Laboratory. I build corpora, annotate texts with LLMs and test how far that annotation can be trusted.

I enjoy problems that need both an understanding of language and solid work with data: information extraction, model evaluation, text analytics and visualization.

Project 02

LLMs and Propp’s functions

How accurately and consistently do language models annotate fairy tales with Propp’s character functions — and does guidance in the prompt help? I compared 5 models across 3 prompting strategies: function names only, with definitions, and with an example. The prompts, code and statistical analysis are my own.

Project 03

LiveLib Emotion Map

A lab project with LiveLib, a major Russian book platform: which emotions do books evoke in readers? Reviews are annotated with a 26-emotion taxonomy using YandexGPT; my share is about 1/6 of an 800k-review corpus. On a pilot sample I built a Yandex DataLens dashboard: emotions by genre and aspect, audience, book profiles, mood-based picks.
